Energy is everywhere.

It exists in the movement of clouds, the force of a storm, the roots of a tree, the heat of the sun, and the movement of everything around us. It exists within us too, in our impulses, ambitions, emotions, movement, and the constant drive to become something more.

The Energy Collection explores that invisible force through physical form.

Each piece transforms energy into something tangible: large, irregular forms that emerge from the canvas, twisting, spreading, colliding, and pushing beyond the boundaries of the surface. No two works follow the same movement. Some feel controlled. Others feel chaotic. Some expand outward, while others appear to collapse inward.

The materials are intentionally raw. Rough surfaces, layered forms, muted backgrounds, and powerful colors create the feeling of something caught in motion, a moment of energy frozen in physical form.

This collection is not about depicting energy literally. It is about creating a physical sensation of it.

A reminder that beneath everything we see, there is always something moving.
