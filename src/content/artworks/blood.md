A mass of red rises from the surface, rough, uneven, and almost alive. The form feels as though it is pushing outward from beneath the canvas - something contained that has become impossible to keep contained.  The deep red carries the feeling of blood, energy, urgency, and movement. Its irregular surface gives the piece a physical sense of tension: not polished, not controlled, but constantly shifting. BLOOD is about energy at its most immediate - raw, instinctive, and impossible to ignore. It captures the moment when something inside you stops remaining quiet and begins to take physical form.
