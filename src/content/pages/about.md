title: About
statement: Turning Energy Into Art
---

I’m Levi Gagliano, an artist exploring the energy that moves through us, around us, and beyond us.

My work is an attempt to give something invisible a physical form. I’m drawn to the feeling of energy in nature, movement, ambition, chaos, and the constant force that pushes us forward. Each piece is created by hand as an expression of that feeling - raw, unpredictable, and constantly evolving.

This collection is not about capturing one specific moment or emotion. It’s about capturing energy itself: something we all experience, but rarely stop to see.

I want these works to bring that feeling into the spaces they occupy - to become a visual reminder of movement, possibility, and the energy already within you.
